Explore the Night Sky

Each night, the universe reveals a breathtaking display of stars, planets, galaxies, and other celestial phenomena. However, many of these wonders remain unnoticed. This platform is designed to help you discover what is visible in the night sky today, providing guidance to identify celestial objects and learn about them. It transforms stargazing into an informative and engaging experience, making the beauty and knowledge of the cosmos more accessible to everyone.

Tips for Stargazing

1 —
Find a dark location

Far from city lights for the best starry views.

2 —
Let your eyes adapt

It takes about 30 minutes for complete dark adaptation.

3 —
Use binoculars

Or a telescope for hunting deep sky objects.

4 —
Avoid phones

Use red light filters if you must use a screen.
